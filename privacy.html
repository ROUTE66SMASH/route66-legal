1. About This Application

ROUTE 66 SMASH is developing a private marketing management application for its restaurant. The application is intended for use by the restaurant owner to prepare, review, and publish approved content to the restaurant's connected TikTok account.

2. Information We May Process

When the application is operational and a TikTok account is connected with authorization, it may process information made available through approved TikTok permissions, such as account identifiers, basic profile information, authorization credentials, and information required to upload or publish content.

The application may also process restaurant marketing materials, draft captions, videos, images, approval decisions, and publishing records.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to connect the restaurant's authorized account, prepare marketing content, obtain owner approval, publish approved posts, maintain application functionality, and address technical or security issues.

The application is designed to require the owner's approval before content is published.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the application, such as hosting, database, or AI service providers, subject to their applicable terms and safeguards. Approved content and information required for publishing may be transmitted to TikTok.

5. Data Retention and Security

We intend to limit access to authorized personnel and use reasonable safeguards to protect application data and account credentials. Retention periods and deletion procedures will be finalized before the application is launched. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Account Disconnection and Data Deletion

The restaurant owner may revoke TikTok authorization through available TikTok account settings. For requests to review or delete application-held information, contact us at Route66smash@gmail.com. We will review requests and handle them in accordance with applicable requirements.

7. Third-Party Services

The application may use TikTok and other technical service providers. Their services are governed by their respective policies. This application is not operated or endorsed by TikTok.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the application is developed or its practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
